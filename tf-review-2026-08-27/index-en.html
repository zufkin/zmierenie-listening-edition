Who we are

Theology for the present

The Faculty brings Catholic academic tradition into serious conversation with contemporary human, ecclesial and social questions. Study, research, formation, dialogue and service belong to one academic story.

Theology

Biblical, systematic, historical, moral and pastoral disciplines remain the core of the Faculty.

Dialogue with the human person

Philosophy, social work, counselling and mediation broaden the ways in which knowledge enters lived experience.

Church and society

Research, public theology and cooperation aim to serve ecclesial communities and the wider public.

Research

Research that remains readable

Projects, journals, publications, conferences and international partnerships are linked to people and workplaces. Studia Aloisiana and Teologický časopis receive equal visibility and academic weight.

Identity and leadership

Service. Cooperation. Continuity.

Catholic and Jesuit tradition is expressed as a way of academic work: discernment, intellectual honesty, dialogue, service, care for the person and openness to truth.
